# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-arrows-to-circle"  %}Accessibility

The Hegde Lab is committed to making this website accessible to all users, including people with disabilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA providing accessible and inclusive online access to our work.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or have difficulty accessing any content on this website, please [contact us](../contact/) so that we can work to provide the information in an accessible format.
