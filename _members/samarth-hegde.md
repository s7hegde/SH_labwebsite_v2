Dr. Samarth Hegde is Assistant Professor in the department of Developmental & Molecular Biology at Albert Einstein College of Medicine (NY). He is a member of the Montefiore Einstein Comprehensive Cancer Center, the Cancer Dormancy Institute, and the Ruth L. and David S. Gottesman Institute for Stem Cell and Regenerative Medicine Research. 

Previously, he was an Instructor and Postdoctoral fellow in the laboratory of Prof. Miriam Merad at Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai (NY) exploring the epigenetic dysregulation of myeloid cells and their precursor cells in lung cancer through a systems immunology lens. He received his Ph.D. in the lab of Dr. David DeNardo at Washington University in St. Louis (MO), where he studied dendritic cell immune surveillance dysfunction in pancreatic cancer that limits T cell function and immunotherapy efficacy.

Dr. Hegde’s career has been supported by the NCI K99/R00 Pathway to Independence award, the NCI F99/K00 Predoctoral-to-Postdoctoral Transition award, and the CSIR-SPM Junior Research Fellowship.
